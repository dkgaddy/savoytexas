Savoy Male & Female College

History of Savoy Male and Female College, 1876–1890 by Mattie Lee Boyd — a 1939 University of Texas master's thesis — covering the founding and operation of the college, including faculty, curriculum, and its lasting impact on the Savoy community. The full text is now searchable in the archive.

Open PDF

Founded by R.R. Halsell in 1876, the college drew students from across North Texas and operated for about fourteen years before its building burned on July 3, 1890. Its legacy was honored decades later by the naming of the Halsell Memorial Gymnasium.
