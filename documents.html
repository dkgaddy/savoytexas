Savoy Male & Female College

A comprehensive historical document covering the founding and operation of the Savoy Male and Female College from 1876 to 1890, including faculty, curriculum, and the college's lasting impact on the Savoy community.

Open PDF

Founded by R.R. Halsell, the college drew students from across North Texas and operated for fourteen years before closing around 1890. Its legacy was honored decades later by the naming of the Halsell Gymnasium.
