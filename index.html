Timeline

★
  • c. 1867Savoy is settled by the Savoy family in Fannin County, Texas.
  • 1876R.R. Halsell founds the Savoy Male & Female College, bringing higher education to North Texas.
  • 1880A devastating cyclone strikes the town; newspapers across Texas cover the disaster.
  • 1885+Savoy receives its town charter. Commerce, churches, and schools flourish for generations.
