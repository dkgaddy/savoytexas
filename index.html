History at a Glance

Key Moments in Savoy's Story

★
c. 1867

Town Founded

The Savoy family — Col. William Savoy and his descendants — settle in Grayson County, giving the community its name.

1876

Savoy Male & Female College

R.R. Halsell founds the college, which draws students from across North Texas for over a decade.

1880

Great Cyclone

A powerful tornado strikes the town, leaving widespread destruction documented in contemporary newspaper accounts and photographs.

1885

Town Charter & Election

Savoy is formally chartered and holds its first official town election, establishing local governance.

1938

New School Campus

A modern school campus is completed, including the Halsell Gymnasium — named in honor of the college founder's legacy.

1954

Great Fire

A devastating fire consumes multiple downtown buildings including the Church of Christ, reshaping the town center.

About Savoy

A Town With Deep Roots

Savoy is a small community in Grayson County, Texas, located in the northeastern corner of the state. Named for the Savoy family who settled here after the Civil War, the town once boasted a thriving college, active commerce, its own newspaper, and a resilient spirit that endured tornadoes, fires, and the steady march of time.

Col. William Savoy and his family left an enduring legacy in the community that bears their name. The College, the Charter, the families who built homes and businesses here — their stories live on in the photographs and documents preserved in this archive.
