Timeline

  • c. 1867 Savoy is settled by the Savoy family in Grayson County, Texas.
  • 1876 R.R. Halsell founds the Savoy Male & Female College, bringing higher education to North Texas.
  • 1880 A devastating cyclone strikes the town; newspapers across Texas cover the disaster.
  • 1885+ Savoy receives its town charter. Commerce, churches, and schools flourish.
